Our Tour Packages

Choose your prefered destination and enjoy your trip.

  • Sarawak Cultural Village (?)

    A 17-acre living museum featuring authentic replica longhouses representing major ethnic groups in Sarawak, complete with a popular 45-minute cultural performance.

  • Tropical Adventure Tours & Travel Sdn Bhd Miri (?)

    A Miri-based travel agency offering exciting adventure and tourism experiences across Sarawak, including eco-adventures, trekking, underwater activities and nature exploration.

  • Lamin Dana Cultural Centre (?)

    Located in Kampung Tellian, this traditional Melanau longhouse functions as a living museum where you can try Umai (a local spicy raw fish delicacy) and learn about sago processing.

  • Similajau National Park (?)

    Located just outside the town, this park features unique coastal rainforests, untamed golden-sand beaches, and hiking trails that hug the rocky shoreline.

  • Fairy Cave & Wind Cave (?)

    Fairy Cave is a massive, multi-tiered chamber dripping with stalactites, while the nearby Wind Cave features a subterranean wooden boardwalk where you can feel continuous, cool breezes rushing through the dark.

  • Fort Alice (?)

    Built out of heavy belian (ironwood) in 1864, this beautifully restored hilltop fort was built by the White Rajahs to guard against regional Iban rebellions and now acts as a local heritage museum.
